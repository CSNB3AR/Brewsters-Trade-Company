# Crystal League Recruiting Criteria

## Roles Needed

- Tank / Initiator
- Healer
- CC / Support
- 1-2 DPS

## What to Evaluate

- Depth of role knowledge, not just kill counts
- Gear self-sufficiency
- Comms discipline
- Availability matching the locked prime-time window
- Killboard / match history ([albiononline.com/killboard](https://albiononline.com/killboard))
- Team-first mentality
- Willingness to be filmed for content
