# Reference Notes

## Game-Mechanics Corrections (accurate as of this writing)

- Caerleon is the city being referred to, confirmed as the Royal city, red-zone bordered, home to the only Black Market in the game, with a crafting bonus.
- Crystal League is not dead. Only the standalone Crystal League Championship event was discontinued in 2023. The League itself and Arena continue, Season 33 was running as of July 2026.
- Hideouts can be placed in the Roads of Avalon, not only Black Zone territory.
- Corrupted Dungeons are instanced 1v1 content, not territory you hold.
- Individual city building plots still exist via a 4-week blind auction. Full city/territory conquest (20v20 GvG) was removed in December 2020.
- Personal islands have farm plots. Guild islands have zero farm plots, they're for laborers, building plots, marketplace, and the Guild Hall.
- Multiple guild islands are mainly useful for laborer/plot capacity scaling, not farming.
- The Albion Online Creators Program has a video-only entry path (see `content/content-plan.md`).

## Game Health Check (as of August 2026)

- Steam concurrents flat/cyclical: roughly 7,300 to 9,300 monthly average through 2026, with peaks near 14,500 around April/May 2026.
- Stillfront (Albion's parent company) reported Albion returned to 10% organic revenue growth in Q2 2026, crediting the April Xbox launch and strong feature releases.
- Ashes of Creation collapsed in early 2026: founder resigned January 31, mass layoffs, pulled from Steam February 2, ongoing litigation. No quantified player migration from Ashes to Albion, only anecdotal forum chatter.
- Dragonfire content update scheduled for August 31, 2026 (dragons, new realm, raid content).
- No official mobile-specific player count exists publicly. Sandbox's Operations Director stated engagement has been "significantly driven" by the Abyssal Depths and Realm Divided updates, and described mobile/cross-platform as a core ongoing growth pillar.
