# Content / Streaming Plan

## Platform

YouTube first, long-form plus Shorts. No full livestream requirement necessary.

Discord is stood up alongside the channel for coordination and recruitment.

## Short-Form Series Ideas

Two concepts, both Shorts-friendly:

1. **Trade Runs.** Horizontal-format clips of actual route running, black market deliveries, showing the loop (buy, transport, deliver) with some trader-vibe background music. Doesn't need to show the full map, just the delivery itself.
2. **Open-World Kill Count.** Clip-based, not indiscriminate ganking content, framed as a challenge toward a round number like 1,000 open-world kills. A kill counter overlay, some meme elements, the point is showing real open-world fights (1v1s and multi-kill clips both count) building toward the number, not mindless farming for a body count. No confirmed in-game title or achievement is being referenced here, it's a self-imposed challenge, not tied to an official Albion system.

## Milestones

1. Host a small in-guild tournament once Crystal League/Arena play is regular.
2. Apply to the Albion Online Creators Program once there's content and a tournament to point to.

## Albion Online Creators Program Eligibility

The program has a video-only entry path:
- **4 YouTube videos** gets Twitch Drops eligibility.
- **Full program eligibility** requires one of: 2,500 average YouTube views, 5,000 average TikTok/Shorts views, or 50 concurrent Twitch viewers.
