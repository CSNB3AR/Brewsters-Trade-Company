# Content / Streaming Plan

## Platform

YouTube first, long-form plus Shorts. No full livestream requirement necessary.

Discord is stood up alongside the channel for coordination and recruitment.

## Milestones

1. Host a small in-guild tournament once Crystal League/Arena play is regular.
2. Apply to the Albion Online Creators Program once there's content and a tournament to point to.

## Albion Online Creators Program Eligibility

The program has a video-only entry path:
- **4 YouTube videos** gets Twitch Drops eligibility.
- **Full program eligibility** requires one of: 2,500 average YouTube views, 5,000 average TikTok/Shorts views, or 50 concurrent Twitch viewers.
