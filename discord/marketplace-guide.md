**Check the Albion Online 2D Database before you run a route.** It's built on the Albion Online Data Project, real-time community-uploaded prices across every city. You can check it from anywhere, including sitting at the guild base, no need to be in-game to plan a run.

**How to actually run a route:**

1. Check the marketplace site first. Find the item you're delivering and see which city has it cheapest.
2. **Self-Funded (fastest method):** teleport to the buy city with no gear equipped, nothing to lose if something goes wrong. Buy the item, then bring in a mount to run the delivery. Expect to cross a black zone on most routes, that's the job.
3. **Guild-Funded:** the guild brings the capital and the mount. Meet at the location, guild fronts everything, standard Guild-Funded split applies.
4. **Cost-saving hybrid:** buy the item yourself with your own silver first, the guild reimburses you, then hands off the mount and provides escort from there.
5. **Fully self-funded, no guild involved:** gather materials at the guild hall and run the whole route solo.

The guild specifically handles the leg outside the red zone when it's a Guild-Funded run. If that shipment gets lost out there, it's a guild-funded loss covered by company capital, not something you have to personally cover.
