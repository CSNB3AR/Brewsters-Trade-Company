**How to check prices before running a route:**

1. Start in-game: check the Caerleon Black Market directly for what's listed and at what price.
2. Cross-check with the price bot (already posted, it pulls from the Albion Online Data Project) to compare prices across cities before committing.
3. Confirm the other details, funding type, route safety, before you actually make the run.

**How to actually run a route once you've checked prices:**

1. **Self-Funded (fastest method):** teleport to the buy city with no gear equipped, nothing to lose if something goes wrong. Buy the item, then bring in a mount to run the delivery. Expect to cross a black zone on most routes, that's the job.
2. **Guild-Funded:** the guild brings the capital and the mount. Meet at the location, guild fronts everything, standard Guild-Funded split applies.
3. **Cost-saving hybrid:** buy the item yourself with your own silver first, the guild reimburses you, then hands off the mount and provides escort from there.
4. **Fully self-funded, no guild involved:** gather materials at the guild hall and run the whole route solo.

The guild specifically handles the leg outside the red zone when it's a Guild-Funded run. If that shipment gets lost out there, it's a guild-funded loss covered by company capital, not something you have to personally cover.
