# Beginner Guide: Caerleon Faction Warfare

We flag for Caerleon. It plays nothing like the other five factions, so don't go in expecting a zerg army to join.

## How To Flag

Head to Caerleon, click the flag button, and hold still for 15 seconds while it charges. That's it, you're flagged. You'll need to travel back to Caerleon any time you want to re-flag, you can only flag for whatever city you're standing in.

## What Happens If You Lose a Fight

This depends entirely on the zone you're in, not the flag itself:

- **Blue and Yellow zones:** you get knocked down, no gear lost. Low stakes, good for learning.
- **Red zones:** full loot. Everything you had equipped plus your inventory becomes lootable.
- **Black zones:** always full loot no matter what, that's baseline black zone risk, unrelated to being flagged.

Red zone kills also pay about triple the faction points of a yellow zone kill, so there's a real incentive to push deeper once you're ready.

## How Caerleon Actually Plays

There's no big faction army here. Other Caerleon-flagged players can attack each other too, everyone's out for themselves. The real play is small squads (think 2-6 people) raiding captured Outposts, which turn into bandit camps that pay whoever's holding them directly, or third-partying other factions' fights mid-battle and cleaning up.

Because there's no army to fall back on, faction pushes only work if they're actually organized. Solo wandering into it works for learning in blue/yellow, but real pushes need a group and a plan.

## Getting Started Checklist

1. Flag up and get a feel for it in blue/yellow zones first, no gear risk.
2. Join a scheduled faction push (Monday-Friday, 23:00-12:00 UTC) once you're ready for red zone stakes.
3. Move in a small, coordinated squad, not solo, once you're pushing Outposts or third-partying fights.
