# Beginner Guide: Roads of Avalon

A shifting network of black-zone-only pathways separate from the rest of the map. This is where the guild's hideout lives and where high-value static dungeons show up.

## What Makes It Different

The Roads aren't a fixed place, the layout changes constantly and no area is accessible the same way twice. Every zone in there is Black Zone, full loot, so this isn't beginner-friendly territory, get comfortable with open-world PvP first.

## Getting In

Avalonian Portals appear in every zone Tier 5 and above, on both the Royal Continent and the Outlands. Each portal has a set number of charges, one charge is used per entry, and charges recharge every 5 minutes. Portals also despawn on a timer, they're not permanent.

## What's In There

- Hideouts (including the guild's own, once one is placed).
- High-value static dungeons with a significant loot boost compared to normal dungeons.
- Genuine PvP risk, this is black zone content the whole way through.
- Will o' Wisps, the entry point into the Mists (see below).

## The Mists

The Mists are a separate biome reached via Will o' Wisps found along the Roads. It's built as a sanctuary for solo and duo play, low player density, ideal for small-scale fights without the chaos of a full open zone.

- **Entry:** the Wisp portal closes about 30 seconds after it's discovered or entered, don't hesitate once you find one.
- **Session length:** roughly 20 minutes per Mist, plus a 5-minute grace period to chain into the next Wisp before it's gone. Four side exits exist per instance.
- **Wisp rarity, by trail color:** White, Green, Blue, Purple, Gold, in ascending order. Better color means better loot inside.
- **Knightfall Abbey:** the one static dungeon found in the Mists, same fixed 9-building layout every time, scales T5-T8. Its portal only lasts 2 minutes once found.
- **Crystal Spiderlings:** function like upgraded loot chests, some of the best loot in the Mists, exclusive to Black Zone Mists and Greater Mists specifically.
- **Deep Mists (2026 update):** a newer, higher-risk layer of the Mists. Tier 9 resources exist only here, and gathering them requires new "Grandmaster" tier tools. Not a beginner destination.

## Getting Started Checklist

1. Don't go in solo the first few times, go with people who already know a route.
2. Bring a replaceable loadout, this is full-loot territory.
3. Learn to recognize a portal's charge count and despawn timer before relying on it as an escape route.
4. Once comfortable with the Roads themselves, start chasing Will o' Wisps for Mists access, watch for wisp color, higher tiers are worth prioritizing.
5. Deep Mists and Tier 9 gathering are end-game, not a starting point.
