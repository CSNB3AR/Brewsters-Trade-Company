# Beginner Guide: Roads of Avalon

A shifting network of black-zone-only pathways separate from the rest of the map. This is where the guild's hideout lives and where high-value static dungeons show up.

## What Makes It Different

The Roads aren't a fixed place, the layout changes constantly and no area is accessible the same way twice. Every zone in there is Black Zone, full loot, so this isn't beginner-friendly territory, get comfortable with open-world PvP first.

## Getting In

Avalonian Portals appear in every zone Tier 5 and above, on both the Royal Continent and the Outlands. Each portal has a set number of charges, one charge is used per entry, and charges recharge every 5 minutes. Portals also despawn on a timer, they're not permanent.

## What's In There

- Hideouts (including the guild's own, once one is placed).
- High-value static dungeons with a significant loot boost compared to normal dungeons.
- Genuine PvP risk, this is black zone content the whole way through.

## Getting Started Checklist

1. Don't go in solo the first few times, go with people who already know a route.
2. Bring a replaceable loadout, this is full-loot territory.
3. Learn to recognize a portal's charge count and despawn timer before relying on it as an escape route.
