# Beginner Guide: Corrupted Dungeons

Solo content, and one of the best on-ramps into real PvP risk without needing a group.

## How It Works

You fight through a dungeon full of demonic enemies toward a final boss. While you're doing that, other players can invade, and if they do, it turns into a 1v1 for whatever loot is on the line. You can also invade other people's dungeons the same way.

## Difficulty Tiers

- **Hunter (T5):** the beginner tier. No item loss even if you die, roughly 700 IP minimum with a 900 IP soft cap. Learn the mechanics here first.
- **Stalker (T6) and up:** lethal, full loot, higher risk and higher reward. Don't move up until Hunter feels easy.

## Getting In

Find a Corrupted Dungeon entrance out in the open world (they spawn like regular solo dungeons but look more sinister), or buy/find a Corrupted Dungeon Map that marks one for you.

## Beginner Tips

- Inspect anyone who invades before engaging, get a read on their build if you can.
- Fight the dungeon's mobs efficiently, don't waste cooldowns you'll need for an invader.
- Stay on Hunter tier until you've got a real feel for your combo under pressure, the jump to lethal isn't forgiving.

## Getting Started Checklist

1. Run several Hunter-tier dungeons before even thinking about Stalker.
2. Get comfortable fighting while also watching for an invader interrupting you mid-clear.
3. Move to Stalker once losing a fight there wouldn't set you back hard.
