# Beginner Guide: Open World PvP

This is the guild's bread and butter outside of structured content, red and black zone roaming, faction warfare, and just existing in dangerous territory around Caerleon.

## Before You Go Out

- Don't carry gear you can't replace several times over. If losing it would actually hurt, it's not open-world gear yet.
- Keep a mobility option slotted (a dash, a blink, a fast mount) so you always have a way to disengage.
- Keep invisibility potions on hand, they're one of the most reliable escape tools in the game.

## Staying Alive

- Inspect anyone you pass. Figure out fast whether they're a ganker, a gatherer, or just another roamer, don't walk in blind.
- Watch the map, not just what's in front of you. Ignoring PvP activity indicators and wandering into a roam squad is the single most common way new players get caught.
- Know your escape routes before you need them: portals, Roads of Avalon entrances, static dungeons, and neighboring guild territory can all be ways out.

## Fighting Back

Open world fights favor whoever controls the engage. If you're confident in your build and the numbers are even or in your favor, use your combo's setup (root, silence, whatever your kit has) before they get theirs off. If the numbers are bad, disengage, don't force a fight you can't win.

## Getting Started Checklist

1. Run your first few sessions in a group, not solo, until you know the zones.
2. Keep a cheap, replaceable loadout until you've got a feel for how fights actually go.
3. Learn to read the map and other players before you learn anything else.
