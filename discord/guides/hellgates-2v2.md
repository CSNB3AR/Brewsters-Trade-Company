# Beginner Guide: 2v2 Hellgates

Small-scale, high-intensity PvP. Two of you go in, and usually another team is in there too.

## Getting In

Hellgates are entered via a Hellgate Ritual, craftable at the Artifact Foundry, lootable, or bought on the market. Set the size to 2 and choose lethal or non-lethal when creating one.

## Non-Lethal First

Non-lethal Hellgates are the practice tier, no full loot risk. Run these until the mechanics are second nature before touching lethal. Minimum ~800 IP to enter 2v2, non-lethal caps at 900 IP. Lethal 2v2 wants closer to 1100 IP before it's a fair fight.

## The Meta Comp: DPS + Healer

Double-DPS exists but consistently loses to a proper DPS+Healer pair in practice.

**Starter Build: Holy Staff Healer**

Sustain-focused, keeps the DPS partner alive through extended trades. Keep the main heal spell (usually Q) firing on cooldown, don't overthink it early on.

Heavy single-target burst. Broadsword (sustained pressure, some self-sustain) and Dagger (mobility-heavy, punishes a healer who overextends) are the other two viable current DPS picks if Claymore doesn't fit your playstyle.

## Inside the Gate

- You'll fight mobs, sometimes a boss, and often another player team.
- A closing zone starts dealing damage over time (roughly 1% of your max HP per second, climbing further as it continues), this is designed to force fights instead of letting people stall it out. Don't sit around.
- As a healer, keep your main heal spell (usually your Q) firing on cooldown, don't overthink it early on.

## Getting Started Checklist

1. Run a few non-lethal gates first, learn the layout and the zone timer.
2. Lock in a DPS + Healer pair with someone you communicate well with, Holy Staff healer paired with Claymore, Broadsword, or Dagger.
3. Move up to lethal once your gear can absorb a loss without setting you back hard, and once you're comfortable around the 1100 IP range.
