# Welcome & Charter: Server Rules

Baseline rules for every member of Brewster's Trade Company, regardless of rank. Read this before posting.

## 1. General Conduct

- Treat everyone with respect. No harassment, hate speech, or discrimination of any kind, in voice, text, or in-game.
- No hazing, no bullying. New and Recruit-rank members get treated the same as everyone else.
- No toxicity or flaming, on wins or losses. Bad fights happen, tear the fight apart, not the person.
- Keep personal disputes off public channels. Take it to an officer if something's actually a problem.
- Don't share other members' personal information without their consent.

## 2. Server Usage

- Use the right channel for the right thing, dispatch jobs in #dispatch-board, crafting requests in #order-board, PvP sign-ups in #muster, general chat in #common-room. Keeps everything usable.
- No spamming, no excessive @mentions.
- No advertising other guilds, servers, or unrelated content without officer permission.
- Keep NSFW content out entirely, no designated channel for it here.

## 3. Voice Chat

- Use push-to-talk if your mic picks up background noise.
- No blasting music or sound effects into shared voice channels.
- During organized PvP (Crystal League, Hellgates, Fight Club), follow the shot-caller. Coordination beats chaos.

## 4. PvP & Group Content Conduct

- No scamming guildmates, ever. That's an instant trust problem, not a warning-first one.
- No ninja-looting or stealing tagged content from another member.
- No intentionally sabotaging or griefing group content, that includes bailing on a group mid-content without a real reason.
- No cheating, hacking, or third-party exploit tools. This gets someone banned from the game, not just the guild.

## 5. Enforcement

Two-strike baseline unless it's a scam, hack, or serious harassment, those are immediate. Otherwise: warning first, kick or demotion second.
