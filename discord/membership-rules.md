# Membership Rules

What's expected once you're accepted, separate from general server conduct. Read this alongside the server rules.

## 1. Self-Sufficiency

Once you're in, you're expected to learn the game and earn your own silver. This guild isn't a personal bank, and we don't carry beggars or freeloaders. Guild funding exists for guild activity (route running, crafting materials), not for handing out silver to individuals.

## 2. Activity & Absence

Members need to be in the Discord server while actively playing.

- Going to be gone longer than 14 days? Reach out to an officer before you go dark, not after.
- If you give notice, you get a 30-day grace period, no questions asked for real-life stuff, deaths, work, whatever it is.
- If we don't hear from you and don't see activity, we start looking at removal to keep the roster reflecting who's actually still around.

## 3. Guild Tax

A flat 5% tax applies guild-wide. This funds the reimbursement pool below and general guild operations.

## 4. Gear & Mount Reimbursement

- Reimbursement only applies to guild-organized runs and events. If you're out there on your own thing, the guild isn't replacing your gear.
- Reimbursement matches what you actually had equipped, it never exceeds that. If you show up to a guild event in gear well above what's standard for it, that extra risk is yours, not the guild's.

## 5. Guild-Funded Eligibility

Before anyone gets access to guild-funded route running, they need at least 5 self-funded runs on record first. Prove you can run the route before the guild fronts the capital.

## 6. PvP Is Not Optional Forever

Trial members can start in blue or yellow zones to learn the game, that's fine, that's what they're there for. But earning PvP fame is a required stepping stone, not something to sit out of indefinitely. This guild isn't for people scared to fight, being Caerleon-based and small-scale is what teaches that.
