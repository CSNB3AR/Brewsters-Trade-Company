# Starter Guild Bank / Warehouse Inventory

What the guild bank needs stocked before active recruiting starts, so new members can start running content and crafting immediately. Focused on what actually gets used day to day, not a long-term spending wishlist.

## PvE/PvP Consumables

- **Corrupted Dungeon Maps (Hunter tier, non-lethal):** ~100, quantity to be confirmed. Hunter is the beginner/non-lethal tier, Stalker and Slayer step up to lethal and higher IP requirements, not needed for the current non-lethal focus.
- **Hellgate Rituals (2v2, non-lethal):** ~100, quantity to be confirmed. Craftable at the Artifact Foundry, lootable, or buyable on the market. Minimum 800 IP required to enter, non-lethal 2v2 has a 900 IP cap.

Source: [KeenGamer - Corrupted Dungeons](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/albion-online-everything-you-need-to-know/section/10-albion-online-corrupted-dungeons-1v1-pvp-and-fame-in-solo-play/), [Albion Wiki - Corrupted Dungeons](https://wiki.albiononline.com/wiki/Corrupted_Dungeons), [Albion Wiki - Hellgate](https://wiki.albiononline.com/wiki/Hellgate), [Albion Free Market - Hellgates Guide](https://albionfreemarket.com/articles/view/albion-online-hellgates-guide).

**Confirmed not needed:** Mists require no purchased map, entry is through a Roads of Avalon portal only.

**Not being stocked:**
- **Potions:** not bought in bulk. The leader is personally leveling potion-making, this becomes self-supplied over time, not a guild-bank line item.

Split logic for using these is already defined in `discord/dispatch-board-rules.md`: guild supplies materials, crafter supplies skill/spec, profit splits 50/50 on sale.

## Guild Island / Hall Upgrades (Deferred, Reference Only)

Not part of the starter kit. The base Guild Island purchase, Level 1, already includes a storage chest and 4 concrete plots, enough to place a Guild Hall, so the functional basics exist without extra spend.

A fully upgraded island runs 6 levels total, roughly 27,462,500 silver, built from wood, stone, and stone blocks. Level 2 unlocks the Guild Marketplace. Level 6 unlocks the Guild Stadium and is mostly laborer/customization capacity.

This isn't something to fund ahead of need. Upgrades should be driven by actual guild activity and a real reason to spend, not bought preemptively before there's a membership base using the island. Sky is the character with crafting built up enough to supply these materials whenever an upgrade does make sense.

Source: [Albion Online Grind - Island Upgrade Cost](https://albiononlinegrind.com/table/island-upgrade-cost), [Albion Wiki - Guild Island](https://wiki.albiononline.com/wiki/Guild_Island), [Albion Wiki - Guild Hall](https://wiki.albiononline.com/wiki/Guild_Hall).
